The Pre-Complaint Differential Hold Protocol

The Flaw of Traditional Binary Freezing:

When a bank receives an alert, traditional systems freeze the customer's entire account. If an ordinary citizen had ₹25,000 savings and inadvertently received ₹80,000 from a compromised mule hop, their entire balance is locked, freezing their ability to buy groceries or pay rent, resulting in severe consumer litigation.

Account Total: ₹1,05,000 ➔ Frozen: ₹1,05,000 ➔ Usable: ₹0 (Customer Distress)
The CyberShield Differential Solution:

CyberShield algorithms compute the exact suspicious exposure delta. The bank Core Banking System (CBS) places a provisional restriction strictly on the suspicious incoming delta (₹80,000) while leaving the pre-existing legitimate balance (₹25,000) fully accessible for daily expenditures.

Account Total: ₹1,05,000 ➔ Held Delta: ₹80,000 ➔ Freely Usable: ₹25,000 ✅
